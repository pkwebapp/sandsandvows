Questions

Planning a Goa wedding from another city

How far ahead should we book for a Goa wedding?

For November to February, eight to twelve months. Monsoon and shoulder months often have dates at shorter notice. Send your dates and we'll confirm within a day.

Do you charge travel within Goa?

No. Our team is based in Morjim, so travel and stay anywhere in Goa is included in every quote.

Can we meet you before the wedding?

Most couples plan on a video call and WhatsApp. If you visit Goa for a venue recce, we'll join you at the venue.

When do we get our photos and film?

Sneak peeks within 72 hours, the full gallery in three to four weeks, and the feature film in six to eight weeks, all in your private PIK Connect gallery.

Can guests find their own photos?

Yes. With face search, a guest takes a selfie and sees every photo they appear in. You control who has access.
