Privacy, kept simple.

When you enquire, the details you choose to share are sent through WhatsApp or your email app directly to Sands & Vows. We use them only to check availability, prepare your quote and plan your coverage.

We do not sell enquiry information. If you would like us to delete an enquiry or have a question about it, email sandsandvows@pkphotography.in or message us on WhatsApp.

Our website may use essential browser storage to remember answers while you move through the booking form. It is not used for advertising.

We use Google Analytics, which sets cookies, to understand how visitors find and use this site: pages viewed, the referring website, device and approximate location. It does not receive what you type into the enquiry form. Analytics is on for this browser.
