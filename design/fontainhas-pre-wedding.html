Fontainhas pre-weddings

A heritage look, planned around the light.

Fontainhas photographs best before nine in the morning, when the lanes are empty and the light is soft on the coloured walls. Later in the day it is busy and harsh. Many couples start here for a heritage look, change in the car, and drive north for the cliffs at Chapora or the shoreline at Vagator as the sun goes down.

Good to know

Questions about Fontainhas pre-weddings

What is the best time to shoot in Fontainhas?

Early morning, before 9 am. The lanes are quiet and the light is soft on the walls. By midday it is crowded and the light is harsh.

Do we need permission to shoot in Fontainhas?

Fontainhas is a lived-in neighbourhood. We plan respectfully around residents and handle any location permissions as part of your shoot plan.

What should we wear?

Colours that sit well against ochre and indigo walls: ivory, pastels and deep jewel tones work well. Comfortable shoes for the lanes.

Can we do Fontainhas and a beach on the same day?

Yes. A common plan is Fontainhas for the first look, then a drive north to Chapora or Vagator for golden hour and sunset.
